Your library

Private to your wallet.

Every photo is encrypted on your device before it goes anywhere. Only your bWalletX can open it again.

Connect bWalletX to open your library. Nothing about your photos is visible to us, or to anyone else, without your wallet.

Opening your wallet…

Adding photos opens soon. You’re connected and ready: when it opens, add photos here and they’re sealed to your keys.

    We never see your photos. What leaves your device is ciphertext. Each photo has its own key, and your wallet holds it. Keep your bWalletX backup safe: without it these photos can’t be opened.
