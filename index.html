Opening your photos…

    We never see your photos. Each one is encrypted on your device with its own key, and your wallet holds that key. Sealed like that, your photos are kept in bDriveX, so they follow you to every device where you use your wallet; bDriveX holds only the sealed copies. bDriveX is invite-only for now: until your wallet is on the list, your photos stay in this browser. Keep your bWalletX backup safe: without it these photos can’t be opened.
