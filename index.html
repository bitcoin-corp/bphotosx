Get bWalletX

01

Pick a photo

From your camera roll or your camera. Location data is taken out before anything else happens.

02

Sealed on your device

The photo is encrypted in your browser. Only your bWalletX keys can open it again.

03

Minted to your keys

The sealed photo is written to Bitcoin and held in your own wallet, with a blurred preview in Wallet › NFTs. Your wallet shows the fee and asks you first.

Your photos

Opening your wallet…

Minting opens soon. You’re connected and ready: when it opens, add photos here and they’re sealed to your keys.

    What goes on chain is ciphertext plus a blurred preview: anyone can see that a photo exists, its size and the blur, nobody can see the photo. Each photo has its own key, derived from your wallet. Keep your bWalletX backup safe, because without it these photos can't be opened.
